Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to its use for marketing, and withdraw your consent. Email privacy@awning.one. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

We use one first-party cookie to remember your choice, and analytics cookies (Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager, and Microsoft Clarity) only after you accept them in the banner. Rejecting them does not change how the site works. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer, or clear cookies in your browser.

We use secure hosting, encrypted connections and access limits. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you and the Regulator if a breach affects your information, as the law requires.
